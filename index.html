🗺️ MAP NAVIGATION - MERCATI DI TRAIANO RUINS (36MB DETAILED)

The map is a historical Roman ruins complex with multi-level structures, photogrammetry scanned:

  • Lower Market Halls: Tight CQB corridors, pillars for cover, dark shadows. Perfect for Thompson & MP40 (700 & 600 RPM)
  • Upper Terraces: Open sniper lines, broken columns, elevated advantage. Kar98k dominates with 4.5x scope, 95 damage
  • Central Forum: Large open area, high risk/reward, crossfire zone. M1 Garand mid-range king (48 dmg, 120m range)
  • Archways & Stairs: Vertical gameplay, flank routes, ambush spots, step-up 0.6m for small ledges

Movement is detailed with: momentum preservation, air control (8 accel in air vs 25 ground), crouch sliding on slopes, head bob tied to speed (6Hz crouch, 10Hz walk, 14Hz sprint), weapon bob independent, capsule collision radius 0.35m, 8-direction wall checks.
