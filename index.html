MAP NAVIGATION - MERCATI DI TRAIANO RUINS

The map is a historical Roman ruins complex with multi-level structures:

  • Lower Market Halls: Tight CQB corridors, pillars for cover, dark shadows
  • Upper Terraces: Open sniper lines, broken columns, elevated advantage
  • Central Forum: Large open area, high risk/reward, crossfire zone
  • Archways & Stairs: Vertical gameplay, flank routes, ambush spots

Movement is detailed with: momentum, air control, crouch sliding on slopes, step-up for small ledges, head bob tied to speed, weapon bob independent of camera.
